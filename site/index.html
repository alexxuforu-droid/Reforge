Give Windows a spa day.

Reforge turns your Windows desktop into yours — live animated wallpapers, video & GIF wallpapers, custom sound schemes, font replacement, a taskbar redesigner, lock-screen designer, screensaver studio, RGB sync, desktop widgets, a security center, and a full undo history that makes every change reversible. No cloud account. No telemetry. Everything runs on your machine.

🖼 Live wallpapers

Procedural animated scenes, imported video & GIF wallpapers, per-monitor static images — all rendered behind your icons, all undoable.

🔊 Full sound & font control

Import your own .wav into any system event, save schemes, preview fonts in real time, and swap the UI font — with an undo log the whole way.

🛡 Security center

Defender protection layers, threats, exclusions, controlled-folder access, and a temporary real-time-protection pause — with honest, plain-language status.

📦 Shareable packs

Capture your whole look — accent, wallpaper, video, sounds, fonts, taskbar, lock screen — into a .reforgepack file. Packs are data-only and security-scanned on import.

↩️ Everything reversible

Every change records a before-snapshot. One click in History reverts any single action or a whole pack — even a full-look snapshot restore.

🔒 Local-first

Zero telemetry, zero cloud accounts. Updates are opt-in. Reforge does its job on your machine and only your machine.

Windows 10 & 11 Portable & installer Not code-signed — “Unknown publisher” on first run is expected
